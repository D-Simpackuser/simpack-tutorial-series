## English

This tutorial library describes an existing set of video files and Chinese/English subtitle files. Its catalogue is based on the actual inventory, with stable lesson IDs and original filenames retained. The seven formal Chinese series names were supplied by the maintainer. Topic groups, learning paths and translated display titles are editorial aids based on the source titles; they are not summaries of a complete review of every recording.

The original course author and upstream source have not yet been established. The library does not identify the uploader as the author, claim official vendor endorsement or present the recordings as original work by the maintainer. Verified attribution, third-party notices and permission requirements should be added when supporting evidence is available.

The earlier recorded permission covered uploading the selected existing videos and their available Chinese/English subtitles to the private repository. On 12 October 2026, the maintainer explicitly instructed that this existing repository and its already published content be made public, superseding the earlier keep-private instructions. This records the maintainer's instruction; it does not establish independently verified original-author licensing or grant a reuse licence. Code, documentation, original videos and each subtitle language require separate consideration. No blanket MIT, Creative Commons or other licence is assigned to the entire library. Material with unconfirmed rights remains excluded from new publication.

The eight documentation languages are English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, French, German, Italian, Spanish and Korean. They do not create eight subtitle languages. Display-title translations are editorial drafts reviewed by models; independent review by native-speaking railway engineers is not established. Subtitle availability, format checks and matching SHA-256 values do not establish semantic accuracy, timing accuracy or human review.

The subtitle copies retain the original bytes. No video or subtitle is changed by this documentation update. A complete privacy review of all video content has not been established; inventory checks and attachment hash checks cannot substitute for that review. Software versions, model-file availability, current standards and simulation validation are not inferred from filenames. See [quality and versions](docs/quality-and-versions.md) for these limits and [contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md) for corrections.
